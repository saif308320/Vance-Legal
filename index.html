Wealth Management

Integrated portfolios spanning public markets, private credit, and alternatives — constructed around your liquidity calendar and tax lot history.

We underwrite every allocation against a written Investment Policy Statement. Concentrated stock, carried-interest timing, and charitable remainder structures are modelled before a single trade is placed. Clients receive a quarterly letter from their lead advisor — not a packaged report.

Retirement Planning

Income architecture for principals exiting operating companies — sequenced withdrawals, pension risk, and longevity insurance.

We stress-test spending against 1,000 Monte Carlo paths and sequence-of-returns risk. Defined-benefit plans, deferred compensation, and QDRO coordination sit inside one cash-flow model your family can actually read.

Estate Planning

Trust design, dynasty structures, and governance charters that keep control aligned with the people who built the capital.

Working with your counsel, we map GRATs, SLATs, and private-placement life insurance against state-level tax exposure. Family meeting facilitation is included — so the next generation inherits a process, not a surprise.

Investment Advisory

Independent manager selection, co-investments, and direct deals — no proprietary product shelf, no kickbacks.

Our research desk covers 180+ managers across long/short equity, private equity, real assets, and venture secondaries. Access is earned through diligence, not marketing dinners.

Tax Strategy

Harvesting, entity selection, and cross-border reporting coordinated with your CPA — before year-end, not after.

We run tax-lot level optimization across taxable accounts, K-1 estimates by October, and QSBS stacking for founder liquidity. The goal is after-tax compounding, not headline returns.

The Firm

Sterling & Vance opened its doors on Park Avenue in 1998, founded by two former institutional allocators who believed private families deserved the same research depth as endowments — without the product pressure of a wirehouse.

We remain independently owned. No bank parent. No proprietary funds. Advisors are compensated on client outcomes and tenure, not product placement. That structure is not a slogan; it is the operating system of the firm.

Client-first is not a values poster. It is the only way a 27-year relationship survives a market cycle.

The Process

Four movements. One mandate.

01

Discovery

Balance-sheet mapping, family governance, liquidity events on the horizon, and the constraints you will not put in a questionnaire. Two to four working sessions. Nothing is sold.

02

Strategy

A written Investment Policy Statement, tax overlay, and estate sketch. You see the trade-offs — concentration vs. diversification, income vs. growth — before capital moves.

03

Implementation

Manager selection, transition of existing holdings with tax-lot care, and custody at an independent prime broker. You retain title. We retain accountability.

04

Ongoing Review

Quarterly letters, annual policy reset, and a direct line to your partner. Markets change. The mandate is reviewed, never assumed.
